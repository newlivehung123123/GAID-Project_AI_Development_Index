Six Measurement Pillars

P1 — Research & Innovation

Publications, citation impact, patents, and responsible-AI research output.

P2 — Talent & Skills

AI talent concentration, skill penetration, and AI hiring demand.

P3 — Governance & Regulation

Digital-government capacity (World Bank GovTech) and AI legislative activity.

P4 — AI Economy & Investment

Private AI investment and newly funded AI companies.

P5 — Infrastructure & Compute

Frontier compute concentration: national training compute and cluster power.

P6 — Responsible AI & Society

Responsible AI capacity (GIRAI), public attitudes, and ICT security.

Pillars are formative composites with nested equal weights; scores are min–max normalised within each annual edition.
